⌂OUR PURPOSE

Formed by Christ.

Steady Men exists to help everyday men be intentionally formed by Christ through daily Scripture, honest brotherhood, prayer, and accountability. As our hearts are pointed toward God, our lives should show the evidence of that formation in our homes, churches, workplaces, and communities.

OPTIONAL | LEADING AT HOME

Scripture in everyday life.

This is not a main requirement of the group, and it is not meant to add pressure or create another thing to keep up with. It is simply an optional tool for men who want to share the daily Scripture reading with their wife, family, kids, or even a friend.

This connects with Deuteronomy 6:5-9, where God's Word is not only something to know privately, but something carried in the heart and talked about in the normal rhythms of life.
